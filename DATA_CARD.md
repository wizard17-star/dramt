## Sentiment (GDELT 2.0 DOC API + FinBERT scoring in M2)

**Known limitation (documented explicitly per project instructions):** the GDELT 2.0 DOC API enforces a strict rate limit (observed: HTTP 429 even with 30-60s gaps between requests on this environment's shared egress IP). To keep the full 2016-01-01-present pull tractable, article queries use two resolutions:
- **Daily** queries inside the sentiment-equity case study window (2022-12-01 to 2023-03-31).
- **Monthly** queries for the remainder of the full historical span (coarser sentiment resolution outside the case study window; per-day sentiment features outside this window are forward-filled from the enclosing month's aggregate in M2).

GDELT's DOC 2.0 full-text search index also has materially better English-language coverage from ~2017 onward; article volume for 2016 may be sparse.

## Missingness policy

Days/windows with zero retrieved news articles are filled with neutral sentiment (signed_score = 0) plus an explicit `has_news=0` missingness flag, applied in M2 (`src/data/sentiment_gdelt_finbert.py`), not at raw-download time.
